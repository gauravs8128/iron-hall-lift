# Premium Dark Gym Redesign

## Direction
- Replace the bright green-led look with a near-black, white, smoked-glass palette and restrained dark cherry-red accents.
- Keep the strong editorial typography and training photography, while increasing whitespace, contrast, and luxury-club detailing.
- Use square-to-softly-rounded geometry with thin metallic borders, avoiding both overly curved and aggressively sharp styling.

## Experience
- Add a short cinematic opening sequence that reveals the Ironvault wordmark, then clears into the homepage.
- Introduce selective horizontal “running blocks” for club disciplines, training statistics, and membership highlights.
- Add polished scroll-reveal, image depth, navigation transitions, and tactile button/card motion with reduced-motion support.
- Refine the mobile menu and all responsive layouts so the experience remains premium on smaller screens.

## Content and Structure
- Preserve the current sections: opening, club standard, training, membership, visit, and footer.
- Restyle each section into a more editorial, premium WordPress-style fitness presentation without adding unrelated features.
- Keep all actions functional and retain current metadata and accessibility basics.

## Validation
- Check the complete page on desktop and mobile for overflow, overlaps, opening-animation behavior, interactions, and browser errors.
